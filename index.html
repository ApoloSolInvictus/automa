Automation Playbook

Start with high-volume work that is easy to measure and improve.

How to choose: begin with a repetitive process that has a clear trigger, a known owner, and a measurable outcome. The time ranges below are planning estimates; compare them with your own baseline before launch.
Highest impact
Ticket & Email Triage

Classify incoming requests, assign the right queue or agent, and set priority automatically.

Planning impact: 5–20+ hours/week
Prerequisites: consistent tags or CRM fields
Measure: assignment time, first response, backlog
Fast to launch
Smart Autoresponders

Send context-aware replies with approved templates, knowledge links, and a clear next step.

Planning impact: 3–15 hours/week
Prerequisites: common questions and approved answers
Measure: deflection, quality, satisfaction
High leverage
Meeting Scheduling

Let customers or prospects request a time, check availability, and receive confirmations automatically.

Planning impact: fewer scheduling handoffs
Prerequisites: calendar rules and booking link
Measure: booking time, no-shows, conversion
Always on
Data Sync & Entry

Move approved customer, lead, and contract data between the tools your team already uses.

Planning impact: removes duplicate entry
Prerequisites: field mapping and a source of truth
Measure: sync failures, duplicate records, lag
Easy to measure
Scheduled Reports

Gather workflow activity, summarize the key changes, and send a report to the right people on schedule.

Planning impact: removes recurring manual reporting
Prerequisites: trusted data and report recipients
Measure: delivery time, action rate, exceptions
Automa focus
Contract Follow-up

Planning impact: fewer stalled handoffs
